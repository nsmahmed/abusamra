    Using the twin

    A live discrete-event simulation of every truck through Abu Samra, on a true-scale 3D model of the port.

    1. Look around. Left-drag orbits, right-drag pans, the wheel zooms. Click any truck to follow it.
    2. Change the port. Open the planner [ and move a slider. New lanes, bays and ramps appear gold on the map; removed ones turn red.
    3. Compare a full day. Run the 24-hour forecast to see time in port, queues and CO₂ against the baseline.
    4. Watch it live. The live panel ] shows occupancy, plate reads, and the journey of any truck.
    5. Control time. Pick a speed (space pauses) and choose day or night lighting independently of the clock.
